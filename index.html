About Bhoomika Crafts

Bhoomika Crafts is an art and craft shop that also provides a space for artists to exhibit their paintings and creative works. We bring the physical gallery experience online through our virtual exhibition center, so art lovers can explore and connect with artists from anywhere.


Located at Ukken Arcade, Near Rajiv Gandhi Town Hall, Irinjalakuda, Thrissur, we are a hub for creativity, offering premium supplies and a platform for local talent.
